Research Experience

Aug 2023 – Present

Graduate Research Assistant

University of Florida · Gainesville, FL

Active perception and uncertainty representations in GPS-denied environments (Advisor: Jane (Jaejeong) Shin)

  • Developed an uncertainty-aware magnetic navigation planner that, compared with state-of-the-art methods, improves localization accuracy by 5%, cuts failure rates by 34%, and reduces computational costs by over 99% [J1].
  • Built neural and Bayesian neural network models of magnetometer sensing and magnetic fields, including expandable maps with competitive calibration to Gaussian process baselines [C6, R1].
  • Characterized how gradients in scalar fields determine localizability and used this for information-driven planning [R2].
  • Designed research drones for information-aware guidance; built a magnetic navigation simulator in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Gazebo, and ROS2 [C6].
  • Developed low- and high-level software for an underwater vehicle (thrust mapping, filtering, closed-loop control) and a gesture-driven diver-following controller with transformer-based gesture recognition [C3].
  • Built radar–vision fusion tracking (YOLO detection, global nearest-neighbor association) for the U.S. Navy NSWC Crane Robust AI Test Event (RAITE), evaluated under adversarial attack [C5].
Sep 2021 – Aug 2023

Lab Assistant

Center for Intelligent Machines and Robots, University of Florida · Gainesville, FL

Research on mechanically connected systems to evaluate kinematic feasibility (Advisor: Carl D. Crane III)

  • Automated a 1/10th vehicle platform with GNSS and LiDAR navigation; implemented ROS2 path planning, SLAM, LiDAR mapping and localization, and UKF-based sensor fusion.
  • M.S. thesis (Advisor: Carl D. Crane III): mechanical network analysis of closed-loop mechanisms (four-bar, Stephenson, and parallel) for geometric mobility, joint displacement, and internal joint preload.
